Section 7: Citizen Declaration & Legal Undertaking

Legal Undertaking under the Code of Criminal Procedure

1. I hereby solemnly affirm and declare that the statements made in this electronic complaint are true and correct to the best of my knowledge and belief.
2. I acknowledge that filing a false, malicious, or fabricated complaint is a punishable offence under Section 182 and Section 211 of the Indian Penal Code, attracting imprisonment and legal penalties.
3. I agree to cooperate with investigating officers and present original documentary evidence if requested during inquiry.
