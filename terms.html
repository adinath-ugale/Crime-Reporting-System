Law Enforcement Notice • Applicable to all registered complainants

PENAL PROVISION WARNING: Filing a false FIR or deliberately supplying fictitious statements with intent to mislead public servants is a cognizable criminal offence under Section 182 and Section 211 of the Indian Penal Code (IPC), attracting imprisonment of up to 2 to 7 years along with punitive financial fines.

1. Electronic Grievance Registration

Submission of an online complaint on the CRS portal constitutes a formal preliminary intimation of an incident. The jurisdictional police station reserves the statutory right to verify complainant credentials, inspect provided digital evidence, and conduct preliminary enquiries prior to formal First Information Report (FIR) registration in accordance with the Code of Criminal Procedure (CrPC).

2. Emergency Limitation Disclaimer

The electronic complaint lodging portal is intended for non-immediate reporting and systematic docket management. In circumstances involving active violent assaults, ongoing armed robberies, medical crises, or immediate life endangerment, citizens must immediately dial the emergency police dispatch at 112 or 100.

3. Evidence Authenticity

Complainants uploading digital documents, photos, audio, or video files warrant that such materials are unmanipulated and authentic. Tampering with evidence or uploading deepfake / artificially doctored materials is subject to prosecution under the Information Technology Act.

4. Jurisdiction & Zero FIR Policy

Under national police guidelines, no citizen complaint may be rejected merely on grounds of territorial jurisdiction. If an incident falls outside the territorial limits of the selected police station, the system facilitates internal Zero FIR transfer to the appropriate jurisdictional authority without requiring redundant citizen filing.
