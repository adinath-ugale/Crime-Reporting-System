About the Crime Reporting System

Bridging the communication gap between citizens and law enforcement agencies through transparent digital grievance redressal, real-time investigation auditing, and accountable civic governance.

Transforming Public Grievance Redressal

The Crime Reporting System (CRS) is designed as a centralized digital infrastructure connecting citizens directly with jurisdictional police stations, station in-charges, and state law enforcement supervisory commands.

By standardizing electronic complaint lodging, cataloging digital evidence metadata, and maintaining immutable case diaries with chronological timestamps, CRS ensures that every registered report receives appropriate legal attention without geographical hurdles or procedural opacity.
