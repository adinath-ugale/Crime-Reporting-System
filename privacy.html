Statutory Policy

Privacy & Data Protection Policy

Effective Date: 2026-01-01 • Version 2.4

1. Scope and Objective

The Crime Reporting System (CRS) is committed to protecting the privacy, identity, and personal records of citizens filing electronic grievances. This policy sets out the framework under which complainant particulars, suspect records, digital evidence metadata, and investigation notes are maintained and secured.

2. Information Collected

We collect personal identity details (full legal name, email address, verified 10-digit mobile number, and residential address) solely to authenticate the identity of complainants, prevent fictitious reporting, and facilitate legal police correspondence.

3. Evidence and Investigation Confidentiality

All attached evidence files, photographs, videos, and narrative descriptions are designated as official law enforcement inquiry records. Access is restricted to authorized jurisdictional police officers, investigating personnel, and supervisory state headquarters commanders under strict role-based access control.

4. Protection of Vulnerable Complainants

Complaints concerning harassment, domestic offences, and sensitive criminal matters are handled under restricted confidentiality directives in full compliance with the Code of Criminal Procedure. Complainant identities are shielded from unauthorized public disclosures.

5. Data Retention & Integrity

Case records, chronological timestamps, and official investigation diaries are retained in the official crime repository as statutory audit documentation. For queries regarding your personal data records, contact your jurisdictional police station or the State Headquarters Grievance Officer.
