Standard Operating Procedure

How Complaint Processing Works

A transparent 5-step operational protocol from citizen submission to case resolution.

1

Register Identity

Citizens authenticate with mobile & address credentials to ensure authentic complaints.

2

Submit Complaint

Fill structured particulars, date, location, suspect description, and attach evidence.

3

Station Verification

Jurisdictional station verifies report validity and confirms jurisdictional alignment.

4

Active Investigation

An assigned officer logs field inquiries, evidence forensics, and case diary updates.

5

Resolution & Close

Official closure report generated with citizen notification and permanent audit record.

Legal Notice on False Reporting

Filing a false report or furnishing intentionally misleading statements is a punishable offence under Sections 182 and 211 of the Indian Penal Code (IPC), attracting strict legal prosecution. Please ensure all lodged particulars are verified and authentic.
